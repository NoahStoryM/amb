* Introduction

John McCarthy's ambiguous operator for Racket.

All =amb= operations must occur within the dynamic extent of a search,
which =in-amb= starts; it lazily produces the answers as a stream.

This is version 3.  It is not compatible with version 2; see
[[#changes-from-version-2][Changes from Version 2]].

* Usage

#+begin_src racket
(require amb)
#+end_src

** Basic Choice

#+begin_src racket
(for/list ([x (in-amb (amb 1 2 3))])
  x)
;; => '(1 2 3)
#+end_src

** Pruning with =(amb)=

Calling =amb= with no arguments fails, causing the search to
backtrack; =(amb-fail)= does the same.  Use it to prune the
computations that violate a constraint:

#+begin_src racket
(for/list ([x (in-amb (let ([n (amb 1 2 3 4 5)])
                        (unless (odd? n) (amb))
                        (* n n)))])
  x)
;; => '(1 9 25)
#+end_src

** Iteration with =for/amb=

=for/amb= treats each iteration of the loop body as an alternative:

#+begin_src racket
(for/list ([x (in-amb (for/amb ([c "hello"]) c))])
  x)
;; => '(#\h #\e #\l #\l #\o)
#+end_src

The body is part of the search; the clauses are not.  A choice, a
failure or a pause in a clause is an error.

** Search Strategy

The strategy is a /merger/: a procedure that merges the events of two
alternatives.  =current-amb-merger= is read where a choice is made, so
different choices of one search can use different mergers.  =amb/merge=
has four:

| =amb-append=     | all of the first alternative, then the second: depth-first (the default) |
| =amb-interleave= | the turn passes on at each pause                                         |
| =amb-alternate=  | the turn passes on at each event, an answer too                          |
| =amb-level=      | all alternatives run up to their next pause: breadth-first by pauses     |

#+begin_src racket
(require amb/merge)

(for/list ([x (in-amb (amb (amb 1 2 3) (amb 'a 'b 'c)))])
  x)
;; => '(1 2 3 a b c)

(parameterize ([current-amb-merger amb-alternate])
  (for/list ([x (in-amb (amb (amb 1 2 3) (amb 'a 'b 'c)))])
    x))
;; => '(1 a 2 b 3 c)
#+end_src

** Pauses and Fairness

=(amb-pause)= is where a merger may pass the turn on to another
alternative.  A computation that runs for ever without an answer and
without a pause keeps the turn, whatever the merger: a fair search
needs pauses in the program.

#+begin_src racket
(define (pythagorean)
  (define a (for/amb ([n (in-naturals 1)]) (amb-pause) n))
  (define b (for/amb ([n (in-naturals 1)]) (amb-pause) n))
  (define c (for/amb ([n (in-naturals 1)]) (amb-pause) n))
  (unless (= (+ (* a a) (* b b)) (* c c)) (amb))
  (list a b c))

(parameterize ([current-amb-merger amb-interleave])
  (stream-first (in-amb (pythagorean))))
;; => '(3 4 5)
#+end_src

An observer sees the pauses when it asks for them:

#+begin_src racket
(for/list ([x (in-amb (begin (amb-pause) (amb 1 2)) #:on-pause (λ () 'pause))])
  x)
;; => '(pause 1 2)
#+end_src

* Modules

- =amb/base= :: the core: =make-amb=, =amb-generate*=, =amb-fail=,
  =amb-pause=, =current-amb-merger=, =with-amb-mark=,
  =call-with-amb-mark=.  Enough to build a language on,
  such as a miniKanren (see [[file:tests/kanren/]]).
- =amb/merge= :: the four standard mergers.  It does not depend on the
  effects.
- =amb= :: =amb/base=, and =amb=, =for/amb=, =for*/amb=,
  =sequence->amb=, =amb-local=, =current-amb-shuffler=, =in-amb=, =in-amb*=,
  =in-amb/do=, =in-amb*/do=.

* Implementation

A search folds a direct-style computation into a /generator/ of
events: a procedure of no arguments that returns a result (a list of
values for an answer, =#f= for the end, a positive integer =n= for =n=
pauses) and the generator of what comes after.  The effects are
=shift= / =reset= on one prompt tag, and each says what it contributes
in terms of the generator =k= of the rest of the computation: failure
is the end, a pause is one pause and then =k=, and a choice among =a=
and =b= is =(merge (λ () (k a)) (merge (λ () (k b)) end))=.

There is no driver and no frontier: the frontier is the part of that
expression that has not been called yet.  =amb-append= and =amb-level=
are associative, and keep what is left of a merge as a flat record
instead of a nest of closures.

* Changes from Version 2

- =current-amb-depth-first?= and =current-amb-fair?= are gone.  The
  strategy is =current-amb-merger=, and it belongs to the choice, not
  to the whole search.
- A fair search needs pauses in the program.  A version 2 program that
  relied on =current-amb-fair?= to search infinitely many alternatives
  does not terminate unchanged: choose =amb-interleave= and add
  =(amb-pause)= to every alternative of an infinite choice and at the
  entry of every recursive procedure.
- =#:empty-handler= is gone.  =(amb)= always fails; a pause is
  =(amb-pause)=, seen by an observer through =#:on-pause=.
- =make-amb= takes one generator instead of =more?= and =get=.
- Effects in the clauses of =for/amb= are errors.

The documentation has the full list.
